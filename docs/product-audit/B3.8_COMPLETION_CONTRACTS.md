# B3.8 completion — contracts, rules and known limitations

Owner decisions of 2026-10-21 (B3.8 product completion), as built. Every rule below is pinned by
the tests named with it. Nothing here migrated, rewrote or deleted existing data (clean launch:
the current data is disposable test data; no back-fill was run).

## 1. Client subscription terms — fixed-day calendar periods (decision 3)

| Rule | Detail | Pinned by |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Whole days. **1 month = 30 days.** A client trial keeps its own day count (14 by default). | `api/coach-clients/_terms.test.ts`, `b38Terms.test.ts` |
| Calendar days | A term is `[startDate, endDate)` — `endDate` is **exclusive**. 30 days from 1 Oct covers **1–30 Oct**; the next term starts **31 Oct**. | `_terms.test.ts`, `civilDateParity.test.ts` |

## 2. Fix C — the intent of a term change is explicit (decision 2)

`coachClients.updateSubscription` operations (input contract):

| Op | Meaning | Ledger effect |
|---|---|---|
| `newTerm` | The client's first package (no term, or "set up later"). Refused when a term exists (`TERM_EXISTS`). | One economic term (new `termId`). |
| `renew` | The NEXT package. A running term is renewed **from the day after its last day** — the server enforces it (`RENEWAL_STARTS_AFTER_TERM` for any other start); a lapsed / ended / cancelled one from the chosen day (default today; a start in the past over unpaid days is confirmed and flagged `backdated`). Refused during an unfinished pause (`RENEWAL_DURING_FREEZE`). | The current term moves to `subscriptionHistory` **with its real end** (BE-3: no gap), `archivedBy: 'renew'`; a new term with a new `termId`. |
| `correct` | The CURRENT package was set wrong, or the client changes package mid-term (`reason: 'changePackage'`). Optional `expectedTermId` refuses an edit of a term renewed meanwhile. | Same `termId`, edited in place; nothing archived; the earlier version is in the event's `before` snapshot. Extension / freeze days are kept on top of a new length. A save that changes nothing writes nothing. |
| `setTerm`, `setPrice` | Pre-B3.8 operations. | Refused `PRECONDITION_FAILED` / `APP_UPDATE_REQUIRED`, nothing written. |

- **Mid-term package change — financial treatment:** one economic term; its price becomes the new
  package's price; payments already recorded stay attached to it, so Outstanding = new price −
  recorded payments. No pro-rating is modelled — the coach sets the agreed total.
- **Idempotency:** `requestId` (one per sheet opening) — a retried or racing request is applied
  once and returns what it produced; two different racing changes: one wins, the other gets
  `SUBSCRIPTION_CHANGED` (compare-and-set). Pinned by `b38Terms.test.ts`.
- **Every term creator uses the same rules:** assign, invite claim (claim day in the invite's
  zone), transfer "new", first package. A "keep" transfer hands the term over without the previous
  coach's price, package or term id (the new coach's term gets its own id).
- **Older history entries** (archived by the pre-B3.8 `setTerm`, which also archived corrections)
  are kept as they are and counted as terms (known limitation; test data only).

## 3. Manual client payments (decision 1)

Collection `clientPayments` (indexes: `uniq_coach_request`, `uniq_reverses` (partial),
`coachId_paidAt`, `relationshipId_termId`; also in `scripts/mongo-init-indexes.mjs`) and the lock
collection `clientPaymentTermLocks`. Router `clientPayments`: `list` (coach own; admin with
`users.read`), `record`, `reverse`, `correct` (coach only).

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Money | Integers of **piastres** (EGP minor units); `currency: 'EGP'` always. Term prices stay major-unit numbers (≤ 2 decimals, validated). |
| Methods | `cash`, `mobile_wallet`, `instapay` — how the coach says it was received. Forma verifies nothing; the UI says so. |
| Ownership | The relationship must be the coach's (active or ended — money can arrive late); a `termId` must be one of that relationship's terms (current, renewed, earlier stints). `'current'` on a pre-B3.8 term gives it its id (compare-and-set). |
| No overpayment at entry | A payment may not exceed what is left on its term (`PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_BALANCE`); payments on one term are serialised by a write-conflict lock, so two racing payments for the last balance cannot both succeed. A free package has nothing to pay. A payment may be "not linked to a package" (no balance check). |
| Never deleted / overwritten | A mistake is **reversed** (a `reversal` record pointing at it; the original marked `reversedById`) — at most once (unique index + compare-and-set). A **correction** is a reversal plus a new payment (`correctsId`) in one transaction; if the new one would overpay, nothing changes. |
| Idempotency | `requestId` unique per coach; a retry or double click returns the first record. |
| Credit | A price corrected below what was already paid shows a **credit** (`overpaid`); outstanding is never negative. |
| Not linked to a package (final review) | A payment with no `termId` is money received: it counts in **Collected** (month, all time, by method, trend) but is taken off **no** package's Outstanding. Every surface says so: the client's card ("EGP X was recorded without a package…"), the payment form when the client has no package to link to, Business › Overview (coverage note with count and amount), the Payments CSV (package column "Not linked to a package"). Linking it later is **Correct** with a package chosen (a reversal + a payment for that package in one transaction, checked against its balance). |

Pinned by `clientPayments.test.ts` (16), `paymentsParity.test.ts`, `ClientPaymentsCard.test.tsx`, `coachAnalytics.test.ts`, `CoachBusiness.test.tsx`.

## 4. Business analytics (decision 5) — definitions

All computed on the device from the coach's relationships and payments (`lib/coachAnalytics.ts`,
`coachAnalytics.test.ts`); EGP only — a pre-B3.8 term in another currency is excluded and the
screen says how many.

| Figure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Booked (this month / all time / per month) | Package prices of economic terms (renewals included, an edited term once, pending placeholders never) in the month of the term's first day. |
| Collected (this month / all time / per month / by method) | Live recorded payments in the month received. Reversed payments count nowhere. |
| Outstanding | Σ max(0, price − recorded payments) over terms with an id, current and former clients. **Overdue** = on terms that have started (payment is due at the term's start); the rest is **not yet due**. Credits are shown separately. Terms from before B3.8 cannot hold payments and are listed as not part of Outstanding. |
| Not linked (final review) | Live payments with no package (`unlinkedMinor`, `unlinkedCount`, all time): part of Collected, never taken off Outstanding; the Overview says how many and how much. |
| Due by month end / Lost / renewals / lapsed / counts | Unchanged B3.8 rules (`businessSummary`, D1 / D2). |
| Active paying clients | Current clients whose current term runs (active or paused, not a trial) and has a price. |
| Renewal pipeline | Lapsed (≤ 30 days ago), ends ≤ 7 days, 8–14 days, 15–30 days — counts and EGP value. |
| Packages | Current clients on a running term by package name ("Custom term" when none), top 5 + "Other". |
| Not shown | Profit (Forma does not know costs), MRR, churn, retention. |

Charts: dependency-free HTML; booked `#6E8FD8` vs collected `#D9730D` — validated on the dark card
surface (lightness band, chroma, CVD ΔE 24.5, normal-vision ΔE 26.6, contrast ≥ 3:1); one axis,
legend, keyboard-focusable months with the values in words, a table twin. Payments failing to load
is shown as partial data (booked figures stay). Exports add a reconcilable **Payments CSV**.

## 5. EGP only (decision 8)

- Every procedure that creates a client monetary record — `coachClients.assign`,
  `updateSubscription`, `transfer`, `transfers.resolve`, `invites.create`, `clientPayments.*` —
  accepts no currency other than EGP: an explicit non-EGP value is **refused**
  (`BAD_REQUEST` / `UNSUPPORTED_CURRENCY`), never relabelled; a priced term is stored with `EGP`.
- The coach's currency picker (Account), the price-sheet and transfer-wizard currency fields are
  gone; prices read "Price (EGP)" and are formatted per language (`EGP 1,899` / `1,899 ج.م.`).
- Left as they were (admin / commercial scope): the Forma plan and capacity-package currencies
  (admin-set, EGP), `auth.updateProfile`'s `currency` preference field (no longer read), the admin
  revenue aggregates. Existing non-EGP test records are not converted.

## 6. Expired / suspended Forma coach — P2 read-only (decision 4)

**Server (authoritative):** `api/_trpc/trpc.ts` gates every **mutation** by an active coach account
whose `coachPlans` document is expired (an `endsAt` in the past, or a status other than `active`)
or `suspended`: `PRECONDITION_FAILED` with `SUBSCRIPTION_EXPIRED` / `SUBSCRIPTION_SUSPENDED`.
Default-deny — a coach mutation added later is covered. Read at the moment of each write (a stale
tab or an offline replay gets the same answer).

| Still allowed (`LAPSED_COACH_ALLOWED`) | Why |
|---|---|
| every `auth.*` (profile, password, sign-in / out, refresh) | account & password |
| `coachPlanRequests.submitSubscription`, `.cancel`; `coachPlans.createTrial` | Forma renewal & recovery |
| `messages.markRead`, `notifications.markRead`, `usage.*` | passive markers |
| `coachClients.end` (release), `transfers.resolve`, `invites.revoke` | protecting the client |
| `contact.submit` | help & support |

`messages.send` checks inside the procedure, after its idempotent replay: a message delivered
before the lapse is acknowledged to the device retrying it; a NEW message is refused and **not
stored**. Media uploads (`/api/media`): only the coach's own avatar. Non-active accounts keep their
own earlier refusal (`ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE` / `FORBIDDEN`). **A coach with no plan document is
read-only too** (owner, B38F-R3, 2026-10-22): `NO_SUBSCRIPTION`, the same default-deny and allow-list
(activation = `coachPlanRequests.submitSubscription`; recovery = `coachPlans.createTrial`, which never
grants a second trial to an account with Forma history). Every creation path writes the account and
its trial in one transaction (`auth.signup`, `adminUsers.create`, `adminUsers.setRole`). Admins and
clients are never affected; a client can still message their coach. Pinned by `b38CoachP2.test.ts` (every mutation in the API called as an expired
coach) and spec 94.

**Transfer-in (final review).** A lapsed or suspended coach can never RECEIVE a client, whoever
completes the move: `transfers.resolve` stays allowed (it protects the client — reject, cancel, or a
lapsed current coach handing the client to a live one), so the receiving coach's plan is checked
inside the transfer's own transaction (`reserveClientSlot`: an `active` plan whose `endsAt` is in the
future). A refusal (`SUBSCRIPTION_EXPIRED` / `SUBSCRIPTION_SUSPENDED`) writes nothing — no
relationship, ownership, slot count, term or archive — and the request goes back to `pending`, so it
can be completed once the coach renews. The same gate refuses an admin's direct transfer and an invite
claim. Pinned by `b38CoachP2.test.ts` (transfer-in, 9).

**App:** one gate (`useWriteGate`) on every coach write control — the message composer becomes a
read-only notice (Copy / Open only), broadcast, notes, check-in requests and reviews, assessment
notes / review, measurements, every package action, payments, transfer requests, library and
package editing, the plan editors (route gate) — with the reason as the control's title; ⌘K offers
no "create" commands. Any refusal the server sends (tRPC or upload) re-reads the plan at once
(`planLapseSignal`), and a term ending while the app is open flips it at that moment. Copy no
longer says "renew to keep adding clients" / "your clients are unaffected" — it says the coach is in
read-only mode, can read everything, and how to renew.

## 7. Time zone (decision 9)

The Account time-zone picker is gone; the device's zone is shown, read-only. Dates and reporting
periods follow the device (`Intl`), term dates follow their own anchored zone (§1). The stored
`users.timezone` field and the profile API are unchanged (an older app may still send it); nothing
reads it for presentation. CSV dates are explicit `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar days.

## 9. Known limitations

1. Pre-B3.8 history entries archived by corrections are counted as terms (test data only).
2. Terms from before B3.8 that were never changed have no `termId`: no payment can be linked to an
   archived one; they are listed as not part of Outstanding.
3. A renewal is refused while a pause is scheduled or running (renew after it, or end it first).
4. Mid-term package changes are corrections without pro-rating (§2).
5. The coach's device zone anchors a term; a coach and client in different zones see the same
   calendar dates but access ends at the anchor zone's midnight.
6. Physical-device checks (R-17) remain open, as before.

## 10. Financial domain boundary (final review, 2026-10-22)

Forma has two financial domains; their figures are never combined or compared.

| Domain | What | Where it lives | Who manages it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Forma ↔ coach** | Platform subscription, trial, renewals, capacity add-ons — **Forma's revenue** | `coachPlans`, `coachPlanRequests`, capacity entitlements / packages, the Forma config | Super Admin (`coachPlans.adminUpdate`, `coachPlanRequests.confirm` / `reject`, capacity actions) |
| **Coach ↔ client** | The coach's packages and the payments the coach records (Cash / Mobile Wallet / InstaPay) — **the coach's business** | `coachClients` terms, `clientPayments` | The coach (Business, the client's package screen) |

| Rule | Pinned by |
|---|---|
| Client payments and client package prices never change Forma revenue (`adminCoaches.list` `trackedRevenue` / `capacityRevenue`) or any Forma billing document | `b38FinanceIsolation.test.ts` |
| Forma billing operations (suspend, reactivate, expire, extend, confirm, reject) never write a client payment or a client term | `b38FinanceIsolation.test.ts` |
| Business analytics read only the coach's relationships and `clientPayments` (`coachBusinessQuery`, `coachPaymentsQuery`) — never a Forma plan, request or price | code (`lib/coachAnalytics.ts` takes only those two inputs) |
| One coach never records, corrects, reverses or reads another coach's client payments | `clientPayments.test.ts`, `b38FinanceIsolation.test.ts` |
| Admins cannot record, correct or reverse client payments | `b38FinanceIsolation.test.ts` |
| **Owner, B38F-R2:** a coach's payment records are private to that coach — no role or permission (`users.read`, `clients.readAll`, an extra grant) reads another coach's ledger; admin reads of relationships and members carry no package economics (price, currency, package name, billing cycle, term note) | `b38FinanceIsolation.test.ts`, `b301Ownership.test.ts`, `clientPayments.test.ts` |
| **Owner, B38F-R1:** Forma revenue = confirmed coach subscriptions (`confirmedAt`) + monthly add-ons bought through a confirmed request; admin grants and one-time add-ons are not monthly revenue; Admin aggregates no coach-client money | `b38FinanceIsolation.test.ts`, `AdminSubscriptions.test.tsx` |

**Decided and implemented (owner, 2026-10-22 — B3.8 finalization):**
- **B38F-R1** — Admin › Subscriptions used to show "Total tracked MRR" = Forma revenue + `adminGrowth.clientMrr` (every coach's client packages). Now: "Forma recurring revenue" only, split into subscriptions and add-ons, payment-evidenced; `clientMrr` removed.
- **B38F-R2** — `clientPayments.list` used to be readable with `users.read`. Now coach-only, own ledger; admin relationship / member reads without package economics.
